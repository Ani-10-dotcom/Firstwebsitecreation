Product details

A 3 litre outer lid pressure cooker for everyday cooking. The aluminium body heats up quickly and stays light enough to lift when full, and the lid fits over the outside of the rim so you can see it seated before you turn on the heat.

Bakelite handles on the body and lid are made to stay cooler than the metal around them. The size suits dal, rice, vegetables and curries for a family of two to three.

  • ISI mark product. Made to IS 2347, from an ISO 9001:2015 certified company.
  • Micro tech vent weight. A precision weight valve on the lid for steady pressure.
  • Heat resistant Bakelite handles. Safe grips for lifting and carrying.
  • Thick base and sturdy body. Food grade aluminium with an extra thick base.
  • Gasket release system. Built in to help release the gasket safely.
